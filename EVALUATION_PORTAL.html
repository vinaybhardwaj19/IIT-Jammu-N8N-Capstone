02 · BANT Qualification
8 Nodes · Webhook Trigger

03 · Dynamic Rep Routing
8 Nodes · Webhook Trigger

04 · Follow-ups & Calendar
8 Nodes · 8-Hour Cron

05 · Proposal & HITL Gate
10 Nodes · Webhook Trigger

06 · Weekly Executive KPIs
7 Nodes · Monday 8AM Cron

SQL aggregation across 7-day pipeline velocity. GPT-4o writes executive briefing delivered simultaneously via Google Sheets, Gmail, and Slack.
